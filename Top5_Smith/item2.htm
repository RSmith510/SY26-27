#2 - Charter Bus



Charter busses are the most elite level of bus. They come equipped with seperated, padded seats, and working A/C. Not to mention the bathrooms located at the back. Charter busses really have it all.

Fun Facts:
  • The Boulder Creek High School Marching Band only gets to use one charter bus per season. It is always the closest competition.
  • Some charter bus models have doubled wheels in the front, back, or both.
  • Unlike traditional school busses, charter busses come featuring seatbelts for their occupants.
