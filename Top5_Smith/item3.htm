#3 - TRAINS!!!!



Trains are a very significant aspect of transportation in North America. They carry large amounts of freight from end to end of the continent. Trains are so cool.

Fun Facts:
  • North America is home to 6 Class 1 railroad lines: Burlington Northern Santa Fe (BNSF), Union Pacific (UP), Norfolk Southern (NS), Canadian National (CN), CSX, and Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKS)
  • There are 2 main diesel locomotive manufacturers: General Electric and General Motors
  • The Boeing 747 serves as the United State's "Air Force One"
